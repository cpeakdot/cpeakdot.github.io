Luckwise is for entertainment only. The app does not measure, change or predict real luck. Readings, scores and tools are not advice of any kind, and you should not base decisions on them.

Subscription

Luckwise Pro is a weekly auto-renewing subscription with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Google Play account through Google Play. The subscription renews every week until you cancel it. The price is shown on the purchase screen and in Google Play before you subscribe.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid being charged. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period.

Refund requests are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.
