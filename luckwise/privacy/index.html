Privacy Policy

Your data

The name and birth date you enter stay on your device. They are used only to compute your daily reading and are never sent to the developer or to any server. This profile is excluded from Android device backups and device transfers.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play, which handles your payment. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat processes purchase data, an anonymous app user id and basic device information; it does not receive your name or birth date.

Ads

When the app opens, the Google AdMob and Google consent SDKs start, to check your consent status and prepare ads, even if no ad is shown. Rewarded ads appear only when you choose to watch one to get extra uses. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and basic device data as described in Google's privacy policy. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices. Subscribers see no ads.

Remote settings

The app uses Google Firebase Remote Config to adjust settings such as daily free limits without an app update. Firebase uses an installation ID and basic device information (such as app version, language and country) for this; it does not receive your name or birth date.

Analytics

This version of the app contains no analytics.

Changes

If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change ships.
